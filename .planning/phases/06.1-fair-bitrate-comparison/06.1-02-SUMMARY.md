## Key Results

### Autoencoder BPP (Entropy-Based)

| Model | Compression Ratio | Geometric BPP | Actual BPP | Reduction |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|------------|-----------|
| ResNet 4x | 4x | 2.0 | 1.53 | 23% |
| ResNet 8x | 8x | 1.0 | 0.89 | 11% |
| ResNet 16x | 16x | 0.5 | 0.44 | 12% |
| Baseline 4x | 4x | 2.0 | 1.77 | 12% |
| Baseline 8x | 8x | 1.0 | 0.88 | 12% |
| Baseline 16x | 16x | 0.5 | 0.44 | 12% |

### Bitrate-Matched Comparison (PSNR)

| Model | BPP | AE PSNR | JP2 PSNR | Diff |
|-------|-----|---------|----------|------|
| ResNet 4x | 1.53 | 25.56 | 28.77 | -3.22 dB |
| ResNet 8x | 0.89 | 23.78 | 25.19 | -1.42 dB |
| ResNet 16x | 0.44 | 21.20 | 22.53 | **-1.33 dB** |
| Baseline 4x | 1.77 | 24.78 | 30.20 | -5.42 dB |
| Baseline 8x | 0.88 | 21.67 | 25.17 | -3.50 dB |
| Baseline 16x | 0.44 | 19.43 | 22.52 | -3.10 dB |

**Key Findings:**
1. ResNet models consistently outperform baseline models
2. ResNet 16x is only 1.33 dB below JPEG-2000 at same bitrate
3. ResNet 8x is only 1.42 dB below JPEG-2000
4. Baseline models are 3-5 dB below JPEG-2000
5. Gap between autoencoders and JPEG-2000 narrows at lower bitrates

## Decisions Made

1. **Quality from quantized latent:** Quality metrics computed from reconstruction of quantized (not original) latent to match what's actually recoverable from the bitstream
2. **Log-spaced quality points:** JPEG-2000 quality values (1-1000) log-spaced for even BPP coverage
3. **Linear interpolation:** scipy.interpolate.interp1d with linear mode for R-D interpolation
